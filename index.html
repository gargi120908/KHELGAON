📋 How KhelGaon Works

1

1. Search Your Sport

Choose your sport and state to find selection trials, training camps, and government sports schemes.

2

2. Check Documents

Read age rules, eligibility criteria, and documents needed (Aadhaar, birth certificate, school bonafide).

3

3. Show Up & Perform

Verify dates on the official portal and report directly to the trial ground with zero middlemen or fees.

💡 Why This Matters

In rural India, millions of gifted runners, wrestlers, and kabaddi players train on village mud grounds without knowing when government selection trials happen.

Official notices are often hidden in PDFs across scattered portals and written in hard English. KhelGaon keeps everything simple, fast on slow phones, and free.
