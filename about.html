The Problem We Are Solving

Rural India has immense sporting talent, yet talented students aged 14-19 often miss selection trials because of four daily barriers:

  • Scattered Notices: Trial announcements are spread across 28 state portals and long PDF circulars.
  • English-Only Language: Official forms and rules use difficult administrative English.
  • Low-End Phones & Slow Internet: Heavy portals fail to load on budget Android phones over 2G/3G.
  • Middleman Fraud: Unaware families sometimes pay agents for trials that are officially free.

How KhelGaon Helps

One Simple Directory

Brings major SAI, Khelo India, Army Boys, and state authority programmes into one fast page.

Works Offline

Service Worker and localStorage save the opportunities list after your very first visit.

English & Hindi Toggle

One tap translates navigation, filters, and guides into simple Hindi.

Under 300 KB & Zero Login

No ads, no sign-up, no trackers, and large 44px+ buttons built for 360px screens.

Our Limitations & Honesty Policy

To keep students safe and avoid misinformation, KhelGaon follows strict academic honesty rules:

  • Student Course Project: Built for B.Tech CSE 1st Semester FoCP (CSE-1003), not an official government portal.
  • No Invented Dates or Phone Numbers: Every listing clearly states 'Check official website for current dates'.
  • Official Verification Required: Every card instructs students and coaches to verify on the official site before traveling.
  • Local Submissions Only: Forms on the Share page save locally in the browser (no backend database).
