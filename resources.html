Free Training Guides & Resources

Zero-equipment home workouts, low-cost rural nutrition, and a printable documents checklist.

🏃‍♂️ 1. Zero-Equipment Home Fitness Routine

Build athletic speed, grip, and stamina using body weight and village open grounds.

Exercise / Drill Target Sports Sets & Repetitions Why It Helps
Hill / Canal Sprints (30m - 50m max effort) Athletics, Football, Kabaddi 6 to 8 sprints with 90s walk rest Builds explosive acceleration and running stride.
Surya Namaskar & Push-ups (Dand) Wrestling, Archery, Hockey 4 sets of 15 to 20 reps Improves shoulder stability, upper-body power, and flexibility.
Deep Frog Jumps (Baithak) Kabaddi, Cricket, Kho-Kho 4 sets of 25 reps Develops strong legs for tackling, diving, and fast bowling.
Plank & Leg Raises (Core Hold) All Sports 3 rounds of 60 seconds Protects the lower back and improves running posture.
Farmer's Walk with Water Buckets Wrestling, Archery, Cricket 3 walks of 50 meters Builds strong grip and forearm endurance without any gym.

🥣 2. Low-Cost Indian Athletic Nutrition

Eat well for sports trials without costly supplements, using local village foods.

Power Breakfast
Soaked Kala Chana + Gur (Jaggery)

Overnight soaked black chickpeas give steady energy and iron; jaggery supports stamina and prevents cramps.

Post-Workout Drink
Chana Sattu Sharbat

Mix 3 spoons of roasted chana sattu in buttermilk or water with roasted cumin for affordable plant protein.

Daily Protein
Boiled Eggs, Dal & Homemade Curd

Two boiled eggs or a fresh bowl of curd with dal-roti support muscle recovery after evening ground practice.

Hydration & Electrolytes
Nimbu Pani with Black Salt

Fresh lemon water with a pinch of kala namak and sugar replaces salts lost through heavy sweating.
